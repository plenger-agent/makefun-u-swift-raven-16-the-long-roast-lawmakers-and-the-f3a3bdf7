A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-swift-raven-16-the-long-roast-lawmakers-and-the-f3a3bdf7) entry: one satirical sentence roasting lawmakers who would regulate AI, crypto or the internet but cannot convert a file to PDF.

## The sentence

> The committee chair who spent the morning drafting a bill to regulate artificial intelligence just whispered to his aide, live on camera, "How do I save AI_REGULATION_FINAL_v2.docx as a PDF?"

191 characters, one sentence. The bite is the contrast: a morning spent writing law for the future of intelligence, undone in one whispered question, live on camera, about the most basic file conversion there is.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the sentence to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (fallback `/output`), refusing to ship a line outside the 1–320 character gate.
